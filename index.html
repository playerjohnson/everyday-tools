Your Files Stay on Your Device. Full Stop.

Other free tools upload your contracts, bank statements, photos and passwords to their servers. You hope they delete them. You have no way to verify.

Every tool on Everyday Tools runs entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript. Your files are processed on your device and never transmitted anywhere. There's no server-side processing, no file storage, and no data collection. You can verify this yourself — open your browser's network tab while using any tool and you'll see zero file uploads.
