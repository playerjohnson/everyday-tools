What Makes a Strong Password?

At least 12 characters, mixing uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols. Avoid dictionary words, personal info and common patterns. Use a unique password for every account.

Password Entropy

A 16-character password with all character types has roughly 100 bits of entropy — it would take billions of years to brute-force with current technology.
