What Can You Encode?

URLs, plain text, WiFi credentials, email addresses, phone numbers — any text up to about 4,000 characters. WiFi QR codes let people connect to your network by scanning with their phone camera.

Custom Colours

You can change the foreground and background colours. Make sure there's enough contrast for scanners to read the code — dark foreground on light background works best. Avoid low-contrast combinations.
