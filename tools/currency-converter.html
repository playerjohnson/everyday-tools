Exchange Rate Source

Rates are provided by the European Central Bank via the open Frankfurter API, updated daily on working days. These are mid-market reference rates — actual rates from banks and money transfer services will differ slightly due to fees and spreads.

Popular Conversions

GBP to USD, GBP to EUR, USD to EUR, USD to JPY, EUR to GBP and USD to INR are among the most searched currency conversions. The rates shown here are indicative mid-market rates and should not be used for financial transactions.

Tips for Getting the Best Rate

Compare rates across multiple providers. Avoid airport exchange bureaux. Use fee-free cards like Wise or Revolut for travel spending. For large transfers, specialist services like Wise or OFX typically beat high-street banks on exchange rates.
